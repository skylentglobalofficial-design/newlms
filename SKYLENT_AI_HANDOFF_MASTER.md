## 3. Strategic Positioning: Where Skylent Wins the Market (`Skylent Ko Sabse Better Position Kaha Milega`)

### 3.1 The Positioning Trap to Avoid
- **Do NOT fight Coursera/Udemy on "Number of Courses":** You cannot beat 10,000 video courses with 6 courses if you position Skylent as a "Course Library."
- **Do NOT fight Scaler/upGrad on "₹2.5 Lakh Live Zoom Coaching":** High-ticket live batch models burn 60% of revenue on sales call centers.
- **Do NOT fight PW Skills/Apna College on "Cheap Video Lectures":** Video-only certificates have zero hiring signal left in the AI era.

### 3.2 The Category Skylent Must Own: **"India's First Proof-of-Work Learning & Career OS"**
Position Skylent in the exact white space between **Passive ₹500 Video Courses (Udemy/PW)** and **₹2.5 Lakh Bootcamps (Scaler/AlmaBetter/upGrad)**:

1. **The Entry Hook (Beats Udemy/PW/Coursera):**
   > *"Don't buy a ₹2 Lakh bootcamp on a sales call. Start with a ₹99 Workshop, get 100% (99 Elixir Coins) back, and solve a real SQL or Product Case Study in your browser tonight."*
2. **The Learning Engine (Beats DataCamp/Educative/HBS):**
   > *"Zero passive video bloat. Master 6 high-demand tech & product tracks through written cases, 60-second Boardroom Cold Calls, in-browser SQL/Code sandboxes, and Socratic AI + 20-minute human TA support."*
3. **The Hiring Proof (Beats AlmaBetter/Scaler/Coding Ninjas):**
   > *"Recruiters don't trust PDF certificates anymore. Every Skylent graduate ships a cryptographically verifiable Proof-of-Work Dossier (`/verify/:code`) with real SQL queries, PRD specs, and AI Mock Defense readiness."*
4. **The B2B Institutional Wedge (Beats NPTEL/Coursera for Campus in Tier-1/2/3 Colleges):**
   > *"Give your college's Training & Placement Cell (TPO) a turnkey Proof-of-Work Lab + 1-Click NAAC/NIRF/Placement CSV Export (`/dashboard/college`) instead of unverified NPTEL video completion PDFs."*
