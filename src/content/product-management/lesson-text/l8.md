# Jobs, constraints, and non-goals

**Objective:** Name the job, the hard constraint, and three non-goals for Harbor Desk.

**Why this matters:** Without non-goals, every adjacent request becomes the product. That is how “a list” becomes Gmail.

**Prerequisite:** Lesson 7.

**Estimated time:** 45 minutes.

**Case:** [harbor-desk-case.md](/content/product-management/harbor-desk-case.md)

## Explain

**Job story** (situation → motive → outcome):

> When [situation], I want to [job], so I can [outcome].

**Constraint** is not a vibe. Here it is given: 2 engineers, 6 weeks, no warehouse/ERP, no 24/7 HQ desk hire.

**Non-goals** are work you will be tempted to do and will refuse in writing. Good non-goals are specific: “file upload,” “@mentions,” “replace walkie,” “SKU forecasting.”

Karthik already told you the failure mode: last project grew from a list into mentions, channels, and upload.

Arun’s merchandising belief (missing SKUs) may be real and still a **non-goal** for this bet if you lack frequency and the constraint is tight. That is allowed. Write it down so merchandising is not “phase 1.1.”

## Worked example

Job (Meena):

> When a customer delivery fails on Saturday, I want to park it where HQ will see it, so I am not the support line.

Constraint: two engineers, six weeks, no ERP.

Non-goals that match Karthik:

1. Do not build Gmail-complete (mentions, labels, attachments).
2. Do not replace warehouse software.
3. Do not build an AI classifier for exception type.

A bad non-goal: “Do not talk to stores.” That is skipping discovery, not scoping a bet.

## Practice

Write:

1. A job story for Priya at 06:30.
2. A job story for Arun on Monday.
3. The constraint in your own words (must keep 2 / 6 weeks / no warehouse).
4. Three non-goals. One must be warehouse/ERP.

Then star the job you would carry if you can only pick one for six weeks.

## Expected result

Priya’s job should not say “inbox.” Arun’s job is closer to “see what broke without screenshot archaeology.” Your starred job should mention the constraint, not which person is senior.

## Common mistakes

- Non-goals that are moral (“do not be evil”).
- Constraint as “be agile.”
- Treating Karthik as a blocker rather than the person who told you the true budget.

## Knowledge check

**Q.** If you list twelve non-goals, are you being thorough?

**A.** Only if they are temptations you would otherwise ship. A laundry list of unrelated products (mobile game, payroll) is theatre.
