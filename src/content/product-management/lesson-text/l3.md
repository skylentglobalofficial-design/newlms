# Product thinking check

This quiz is five scenario questions on lessons 1–2: problem versus solution, outcome versus output, and what the Harbor Desk case can actually support.

It is not a trivia contest about PM frameworks or acronyms.

You must answer all five correctly to mark the lesson complete. After you submit, read the explanation even if you passed — the wording is the standard this course uses in later briefs.

**Not in this quiz:** SQL, Northwind revenue, machine learning, job outcomes, certificates.
