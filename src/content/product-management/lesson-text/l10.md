# Opportunity versus solution

**Objective:** Split Harbor Desk into the opportunity (job/pain) and the solution (inbox), and list two other solutions to the same job.

**Why this matters:** If you cannot name another solution, you have not understood the opportunity — you have fallen in love with a brand.

**Prerequisite:** Lesson 9.

**Estimated time:** 45 minutes.

**Case:** [harbor-desk-case.md](/content/product-management/harbor-desk-case.md)

## Explain

**Opportunity:** the job or pain class worth time. Example: weekend exceptions that have no reliable owner.

**Solution:** one way to attack it. Harbor Desk (shared inbox) is one solution. Others exist:

- A **weekend exception queue** (narrower software)
- A **rota + Monday checklist** (process, maybe a spreadsheet)
- **Give cashiers an HQ email alias** (still messy, but cheaper)
- **Staff a 24/7 desk** (ruled out this quarter)

You are not required to pick software. You are required to show you *could* have picked something else.

Data Analytics would attack a *revenue* question with a table. That is not this opportunity. Do not bolt a dashboard onto Harbor Desk to make it look like the other flagship.

## Worked example

Opportunity: Weekend customer delivery failures have no written owner (HD-03, HD-06, HD-09).

| Bet | Type | Fits 6 weeks? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full shared inbox (“Gmail”) | Software, wide | Karthik says no |
| Exception list with status + owner | Software, narrow | Karthik says yes |
| Weekend on-call rota + Monday log | Process | Yes, maybe no engineers |

The opportunity stays the same row to row. Only the bet changes.

## Practice

1. Write the opportunity in one sentence with no product name.
2. List Harbor Desk as a solution.
3. List two alternatives, including one non-software option.
4. For each, one risk (from the case: mute, unread, forgotten walkie, screenshot Mondays).

## Expected result

Someone could steal your opportunity sentence and build a checklist instead of an inbox. If they cannot, you wrote a solution.

## Common mistakes

- “Opportunity = digital transformation.”
- Alternatives that are jokes (“telepathy”).
- Ranking with fake ROI.

## Knowledge check

**Q.** Is “do nothing” a valid alternative?

**A.** “Do nothing new” is valid if you say the cost: Arun keeps reconstructing Mondays; Meena keeps being the support line. Option C in the assignment is that idea with a rota, which is not quite nothing.
