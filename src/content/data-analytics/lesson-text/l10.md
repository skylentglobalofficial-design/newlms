# Dashboards that answer one question

**Objective:** Design a one-screen dashboard (Google Sheets or Power BI Desktop — your choice) that a lead can read in sixty seconds.

**Why this matters:** A dashboard is a decision surface, not a gallery. If the title is not a question, it is decoration.

**Prerequisite:** Category, region, month, and channel summaries you trust (lessons 5 and 7).

**Estimated time:** 45 minutes.

**Dataset:** cleaned Northwind sales (same rules as lesson 1).

**Tools:** Sheets charts, Excel, or Power BI Desktop if you already have it. Skylent does not provide a Power BI license, a live classroom, or a hosted BI server.

## Explain

Stand-up test: a commercial lead looks at your screen for **sixty seconds**. They should leave with:

1. The question
2. The headline number (KPI)
3. The comparison that explains the KPI
4. The trend
5. One filter they can change

**Layout that passes**

```
[ Title: question in plain language ]
[ KPI: valid net revenue Jan–Jun ] [ KPI: valid orders ] [ KPI: April MoM ]
[ Bar: net revenue by category_std ]
[ Line: net revenue by month ]
[ Filter: region_std ] [ Filter: channel ]
[ Notes: valid-row rule + quality flags ]
```

**Layout that fails**

- “Northwind Sales Dashboard v3” as the title
- Eight charts including a pie of cities
- No filter
- KPI that sums `unit_price`
- Hidden Unknown region

**Colour:** one highlight colour for the KPI, grey for the rest. Do not encode a sixth category with neon.

## Worked example — choose the question

**Good question:** “Where did valid net revenue come from in Jan–Jun 2026, and how sharp was the April drop?”

KPI: ₹8.12 lakh valid net revenue (166 lines).

Comparison: Electronics ≈ 43% of that total (347k / 812k).

Trend: March peak → April ≈ −51% MoM → partial recovery by June.

Filter: region, including Unknown.

Insight box (three bullets, specific):

- Electronics is the largest mapped category; aliases `Elec.` and `electronics` are merged in this view.
- April is the weakest month in the extract; this view excludes returns and invalid units/price.
- Two orders have no region (Unknown ≈ ₹6k). Do not treat Unknown as a territory to close.

**Bad question:** “All KPIs.”

## Step-by-step procedure (Sheets)

1. Put pivots on a `model` tab (not on the dashboard tab).
2. New tab `dashboard`. Title = the question, 20pt, wrap text.
3. Three cells as KPIs with large numbers and small labels underneath.
4. Insert chart from the category pivot (bar). Insert chart from the month pivot (line).
5. Data → slicer / filter on region and channel if your tool supports it; otherwise two dropdowns that drive FILTER formulas.
6. A text box: valid-row rule in one sentence; list NW-10013 duplicate, blank regions, aliases.
7. Screenshot at laptop width. If you must scroll to see the line chart, you failed the stand-up test.

Power BI Desktop path (optional): load the cleaned CSV, hide unused columns, drag the same four visuals. Do not publish to a workspace this course does not give you.

## Practice

1. Write your title question in ≤ 20 words.
2. Sketch the wireframe on paper (boxes only).
3. Build v1 with KPI + bar + line + one filter + notes.
4. Hand it to a friend for sixty seconds. Ask them to say the question back.

## Expected result

A single screen whose title is a question, whose KPI matches ~₹812k, whose bar ranks Electronics first, whose line shows the April dip, and whose notes name at least one quality issue.

## Common mistakes

- Pie charts of 12 cities.
- Dual axis that compares units to rupees as if they were the same scale.
- Default legend that still shows `Elec.` as a separate series.
- No notes, so a lead thinks Unknown is a fifth sales region with staff and a warehouse.

## Knowledge check

Your dashboard has six visuals and is titled “Sales Overview”. What do you cut first?

**Answer:** The title. Replace it with one question. Then delete every visual that does not serve KPI, comparison, trend, or filter.
