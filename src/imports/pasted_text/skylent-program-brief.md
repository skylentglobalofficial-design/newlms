==================================================
1. CRITICAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

A "Program" is a PRODUCT.

Do not make program pages look like simple course cards.

A learner should be able to understand:

What is this?
Who is it for?
What will I learn?
How long does it take?
How is it delivered?
Who teaches it?
What will I build/do?
What certification do I receive?
What support do I receive?
When does it start?
How much does it cost?
What happens after enrollment?

WITHOUT needing to hunt through the website.
