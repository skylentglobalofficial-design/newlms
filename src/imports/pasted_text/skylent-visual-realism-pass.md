We now need to make the Skylent.live website feel like a REAL education company and product platform rather than an AI-generated concept website.

This is a MAJOR VISUAL REALISM PASS.

Keep the current information architecture, routing and functionality that were already implemented.

DO NOT modify:
- backend
- database
- APIs
- authentication
- EnrollmentModal
- ApplyModal
- JobDrawer
- LMS
- existing enrollment flows
- business logic

Focus ONLY on public-facing frontend UI/UX.

==================================================
CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION
==================================================

Remove the visual language that makes the website look obviously AI-generated.

Significantly reduce or remove:
- excessive square/grid patterns
- random connecting lines
- abstract glowing nodes
- generic AI diagrams
- floating geometric decoration
- excessive glassmorphism
- repeated rounded cards
- generic "AI future" visual language
- decorative elements that do not communicate anything
- philosophical/abstract copy when concrete product information would be better

IMPORTANT:

AI should NOT be the visual identity of Skylent.

AI should appear as a PRODUCT CAPABILITY where relevant, especially inside Career OS.

The company should visually feel like a real, established education and career platform.

==================================================
NEW VISUAL PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Move the design from:

"AI-generated futuristic concept"

to:

"Premium real-world education platform."

Prioritize:
- real photography
- real people
- real learning environments
- real program cards
- real course metadata
- real product screenshots
- real instructors/mentors when available
- real institutional/industry logos only when verified
- useful UI previews
- concrete information
- strong editorial layouts
- premium typography
- excellent whitespace

The site should feel comparable in professionalism and information architecture to leading education platforms such as Coursera, upGrad, Simplilearn, Scaler and Unacademy.

Do NOT copy their branding or layouts.

Use the quality bar and information density of serious education businesses as inspiration.
