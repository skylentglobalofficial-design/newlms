Build a completely new, premium website experience for **Skylent Global**.

This is NOT a generic EdTech website, SaaS landing page, university template, or AI-generated startup website.

The website itself should feel like a **next-generation education ecosystem** and should visually demonstrate what Skylent actually does.

The goal is that a student, parent, university decision-maker, or industry partner can visit the website and understand Skylent within seconds — but also feel compelled to explore because the experience is visually exceptional.

## BRAND POSITIONING

Skylent sits at the intersection of:

**EDUCATION × TECHNOLOGY × INDUSTRY × CAREER**

Skylent is not simply a course-selling company.

It creates a complete learning ecosystem where students can:

**Discover → Learn → Practice → Build → Assess → Certify → Become Career Ready → Find Opportunities**

And institutions can use Skylent to build, deliver, manage and measure modern learning experiences.

* **Soft Sand:** #EEE9E1

IMPORTANT:

**Warm White → Education / Students / Institutions / Storytelling**

The colour rhythm should feel premium and editorial.

# 19. MOST IMPORTANT RULE

The website must SHOW what Skylent does.

If we say:

“We have an LMS”
→ SHOW THE LMS.

If we say:

“Students learn”
→ SHOW THE STUDENT JOURNEY.

If we say:

“We provide career support”
→ SHOW THE CAREER JOURNEY.

If we say:

“We have a job ecosystem”
→ SHOW THE JOB BOARD.

If we say:

“We work with institutions”
→ SHOW THE INSTITUTIONAL ECOSYSTEM.

If we say:

“Students succeed”
→ SHOW THEIR STORIES.

Every major claim should have a visual or product demonstration.

The final website should make someone think:

**“I have never seen an education company present itself like this.”**

Then:

**“I want to explore this.”**

Build the experience around that feeling.

Do not sacrifice usability for visual effects.

Premium > flashy.

Product > decoration.

Storytelling > generic sections.

Real Skylent interfaces > invented illustrations.
