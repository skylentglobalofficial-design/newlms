## 1. Institutional Thesis & Five Interdependent Divisions

Skylent is an independent **Institute of Applied Learning** built to develop people who can understand, execute, improve, and defend real work (`Learn · Practise · Work · Reflect · Prove`). Rather than operating as a passive video-course marketplace, Skylent integrates five academic and operational divisions on a single technology backbone (`Skylent OS`):

1. **01 · Skylent School of Learning:** Foundations, first-principles disciplines, structured curricula, faculty teaching, and formative checks across six long-term schools (`Business & Management`, `Data & Decision Sciences`, `Computing & Engineering`, `Artificial Intelligence & Emerging Technologies`, `Industry & Operations`, `Research, Society & Innovation`).
2. **02 · Skylent Applied Practice Labs (`Environment A`):** Controlled, clean-room Simulated Enterprise Units (`Northwind Commerce`, `Harbor Desk SaaS`, `FinGrid UPI Switch`, `BazaarKart Logistics`, `PulseAI`, `Skylent Digital Systems`) for guided trials and independent sprints.
3. **03 · Skylent Fieldwork & Industry Office (`Environment B — Work Assignment Exchange`):** Supervised real-world problem assignments with MSMEs, local manufacturers, clinics (non-clinical workflow only), NGOs, startups, and partner colleges.
4. **04 · Skylent Assessment & Credential Office:** Five-dimension competency grading (`Knowledge 15%`, `Execution 30%`, `Judgement 20%`, `Oral Defence 15%`, `V1→V2 Revision 20%`), Boardroom Cold Call oral defences, and W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0 / 1EdTech Open Badges 3.0 issuance.
5. **05 · Skylent Career & Enterprise Office (`Environment C`):** Employer partnerships, work-sample auditions, apprenticeship pathways (aligned with UGC 2024 Internship & 2025 AEDP guidelines and NAPS/NATS ecosystems), and entrepreneurship support.

## 2. Competency Progression (L1–L5) & 60-Hour Notional Workload Standard

Every flagship Skylent programme progresses through five internal competency levels:
- **L1 — Foundation (Understand):** Explain core concepts and first principles.
- **L2 — Guided Practice (Apply):** Complete structured tasks with pre-flight guidance.
- **L3 — Independent Execution (Execute):** Solve messy, unfamiliar problems and edge cases independently.
- **L4 — Professional Practice (Deliver):** Work under deadlines, stakeholder trade-offs, and V1 → V2 faculty red-line critique.
- **L5 — Advanced Practice (Lead & Improve):** Defend decisions orally, execute partner fieldwork, and improve systems.

### Illustrative 60-Hour Programme Workload Specification
Designed with reference to National Credit Framework (NCrF) notional learning hour principles (30 notional hours per credit):
- **Guided Instruction:** 12 Hours
- **Independent Study:** 12 Hours
- **Simulated Enterprise Work (`Environment A`):** 18 Hours
- **Supervised Project / Partner Fieldwork (`Environment B`):** 12 Hours
- **Assessment, Oral Defence & V1 → V2 Revision:** 6 Hours
- **Total Programme Workload:** **60 Notional Hours**

## 3. Statutory, Regulatory & Truth-in-Claims Policy (Non-Negotiable)

1. **UGC Section 22 & Academic Credit Boundary:** Skylent Global Pvt. Ltd. operates as an independent Institute of Applied Learning and issues verifiable Skylent certificates and Execution Flight Recorders. Skylent does not unilaterally confer statutory university degrees or academic credits; any academic credit award or Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) transfer occurs solely through an authorised partner Higher Education Institution (HEI) under applicable UGC regulations.
2. **Public Exemplar & Sample Benchmark Disclosure:** All sample dossiers on `/stories` and `/verify` (`SKL-2026-DA-00142`, `SKL-2026-PM-00089`, `SKL-2026-PY-00214`) carry an explicit **`SAMPLE BENCHMARK DOSSIER · ILLUSTRATIVE PREVIEW`** disclosure so illustrative previews are never misrepresented as graduate outcome claims.
3. **Two-Tier Faculty Review Service Standard:** Learners receive **Immediate Automated Pre-Flight Feedback** on sandbox and rubric checks, followed by **Human Faculty Red-Line Review** within published cohort review windows.
4. **Closed-Loop Elixir Reward Policy:** Elixir is a closed-loop internal learner reward unit (non-withdrawable, non-transferable, and never used to purchase academic passes or alter assessment scores).
